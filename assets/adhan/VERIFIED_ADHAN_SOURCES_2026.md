## App display rule
Show only verified metadata: muezzin name when known, then country/city/mosque when documented. Do not display sect labels. Keep source, license, attribution and modification notes in the app's licenses screen and registry.

## Packaging rule
Audio binaries must not be copied into the release until the exact source file and license are verified. Prefer CC0 or CC BY. Preserve attribution for CC BY works. CC BY-SA files require share-alike compliance review before packaging.
